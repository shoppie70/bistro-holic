# HOLIC design direction

2026-11-12

## Brief

A small independent bistro in Nakamurabashi, presented with the precision of a restaurant editorial. The actual menu remains approachable. Preserve real dishes, prices, opening hours, address, and reservation destinations.

## Chosen direction

Warm paper, oxblood, and a large Cormorant wordmark. Japanese headings use locally hosted Shippori Mincho at 400 and 500, paired with Zen Kaku Gothic New at 400 for information and navigation. Subsets are generated by scripts/subset-fonts.py. The opening starts with a large brown wordmark on a beige masthead. The dining photograph follows beneath it, with vertical Japanese type on the right and a small photograph of the room on the left. This combines the earlier light opening with the newer Japanese typography and photographic composition. Later sections move between an asymmetric lunch spread, an evening photograph, and a quieter kitchen story.

The homepage entry is led by the paper-colored masthead and brown wordmark. Paper-colored reading sections and the oxblood dinner section then establish a different rhythm, while the Japanese web fonts connect all four pages.

## Motion

The focal sequence raises the brown wordmark into place, opens the dining photograph from top to bottom, then reveals the vertical Japanese lines. Scrolling gently relaxes the image scale. Supporting motion is limited to a few image reveals, link feedback, and the mobile navigation. No scroll interception, forced loading screen, perpetual animation, or external animation dependency. Reduced-motion preferences keep content visible and remove spatial effects.
