# HOLIC design direction

2026-11-12

## Brief

A small independent bistro in Nakamurabashi, presented with the precision of a restaurant editorial. The actual menu remains approachable. Preserve real dishes, prices, opening hours, address, and reservation destinations.

## Chosen direction

Warm paper, oxblood, and a large Cormorant wordmark. Japanese headings use locally hosted Shippori Mincho at 400 and 500, paired with Zen Kaku Gothic New at 400 for information and navigation. Subsets are generated by scripts/subset-fonts.py. The opening starts with a large brown wordmark on a beige masthead. The dining photograph follows beneath it, with vertical Japanese type on the right and the text-only line 「いつもの街に、いつもの席を。」 at the lower left. The small inset photograph has been removed so the opening uses a single main image. Later sections move between an asymmetric lunch spread, an evening photograph, and a quieter kitchen story.

The homepage entry is led by the paper-colored masthead and brown wordmark. Paper-colored reading sections and the oxblood dinner section then establish a different rhythm, while the Japanese web fonts connect all four pages. The lunch spread uses warmer paper and an upward-sloping entry. Dinner begins with the opposite slope into oxblood. Each edge carries a fine line; all text and photographs remain level.

## Motion

The focal sequence raises the brown wordmark into place, opens the dining photograph from top to bottom, then reveals the vertical Japanese lines. Scrolling gently relaxes the image scale. Lunch and dinner photographs fade in with a shallow diagonal crop that opens to the full image. The dinner heading follows slightly later. Image decoding completes before a reveal starts, and a revision counter prevents pending decodes from undoing a changed motion preference. Keyboard focus immediately reveals any pending content. Other supporting motion is limited to link feedback, a few image reveals, and the mobile navigation. No scroll interception, forced loading screen, perpetual animation, or external animation dependency. Reduced-motion preferences keep content visible and remove spatial effects.
